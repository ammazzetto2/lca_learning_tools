In this example, converting 10 ha gives 8,000 kg CO₂. Equal allocation assigns 400 kg CO₂ per year for 20 years, including the conversion year.

Annual allocated emissions

Equal allocation assigns the same amount each year. Linear declining allocation assigns more to earlier years and less to later years. Both allocate the same total.

Assumptions and calculation

Illustrative accounting simulator, not a validated LCA calculation. A fixed reference emission factor of 800 kg CO₂/ha is applied to every deforestation event (total emissions = deforested area × 800 kg CO₂/ha). This is an illustrative assumption, not a measured or recommended emission factor. Linear declining means allocation weights 20, 19, …, 1, normalised to 100%; it does not represent a measured physical emission trajectory. Years are inclusive.
